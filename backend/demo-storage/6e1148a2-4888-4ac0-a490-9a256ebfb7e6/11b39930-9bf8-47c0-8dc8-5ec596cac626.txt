Acme Corporation Employee Handbook

Annual Leave
Employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues
monthly and unused days may be carried over into the following year up to a
maximum of 5 days. Requests must be submitted to your line manager at least two
weeks in advance through the internal portal.

Parental Leave
Employees receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. The payroll department
processes parental leave claims within 14 days of submission.

Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require written approval from a director.

Expenses
Business expenses under 100 dollars may be approved by a line manager. Anything
above that threshold requires finance team approval before the spend occurs.
